Hi, I'm Pradnya 👋🏻. I build software that helps robots **navigate, perceive, and work reliably** in the real world.

### About me

I'm a Robotics Software Engineer with a Master's in [Robotics Engineering](https://www.wpi.edu/academics/departments/robotics-engineering) from [WPI](https://wpi.edu). My work spans **robot navigation**, **perception**, and **system integration**.

### What I bring 👩🏻‍💻

- **End-to-end ownership**: from the first line of code to the system running in the field
- **Fast ramp-up**: comfortable digging into unfamiliar code and picking up new tools
- **Team-first**: I step in wherever the team needs it

### Why robotics? 🚀

I love building software that doesn't just run, but moves, senses, and solves problems in the physical world, especially making robots reliable when conditions aren't perfect, because that's when they matter.
